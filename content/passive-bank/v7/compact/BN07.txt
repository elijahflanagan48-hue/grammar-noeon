## D2. Active or passive?

The bracket gives only the verb; the answer may be **active or passive**. **40–50% of all gaps in the batch are active.**

- **basic:** Present Simple and Past Simple only. Easy pairs: `The bus (to arrive) late, so we (to send) a message to the teacher.` → **arrived · sent**. Intransitive verbs (`arrive, happen, come, go, stay, start, end, fall, wait`) give active answers; a doer-less action gives passive: `My bag (to find) in the park.` → **was found**.
- **medium:** all tenses of basic and medium; one text may contain both voices of one verb (`The shop (to close) at six. Yesterday it (to close) early because of the snow.` → **closes · was closed**).
- **advanced:** include Perfect tenses and verbs where both voices are possible but only one fits the information order.
- The context must make the voice unambiguous. In `mini`, at least one gap is active and one is passive.
- **Mistakes (mc/fix):** `was happened`, `was arrived`, `was disappeared`, `is belonged to`, `The prices were risen`, `I was explained the rule`.
