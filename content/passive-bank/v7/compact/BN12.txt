## D3. Modal passive

- **basic:** only `can, must, should, will` + `be + V3`, present meaning. Rules and notices: `Phones (must / to switch off) in the exam.` → **must be switched off**; `Shoes (can / to leave) at the door.` → **can be left**. Include negatives (`can't be used`, `mustn't be opened`).
- **medium:** add `may, might, could, would, have to, had to, ought to`; present and future meaning. No perfect modals.
- **advanced:** perfect modals (`must/might/can't/should have been + V3`) and `will have to be`, `is going to have to be`; deduction and criticism about the past.
- The modal is in the bracket: `(must / to wear)`. At least 20% negatives.
- **Mistakes (mc/fix):** `must done`, `must to be done`, `should have be told`, `can't been seen`, `is going to repaired`.
