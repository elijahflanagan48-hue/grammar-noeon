## D9. State, action and the limits of the passive (medium and advanced)

- **medium:** `The shop is closed` (state) vs `is being closed` (action); `I was born in…` (never `I born`); `The car needs washing` / `needs to be washed`; verbs with no passive (`have, fit, suit, lack, resemble`).
- **advanced:** state vs action with `been`, `The door was locked when I got there`, `get/be + V3`, subtle choices.
- **Mistakes:** `I am born`, `The shirt is suiting you`, `The bag is belonged to me`, `needs wash`.
