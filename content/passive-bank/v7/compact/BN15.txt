## D4. Infinitive and -ing forms (medium and advanced only)

- **medium:** `to be + V3` after want, need, hope, expect, would like, refuse; simple `being + V3` after like, hate, love, enjoy, without, before, after. The voice is decided by meaning: `Nobody likes (to tell) what to do.` → **to be told / being told**; `I want (to tell) the truth.` → **to tell** (active). Examples: `The children hope (to invite) to the party.` → **to be invited**.
- **advanced:** also `to have been + V3`, `having been + V3`, `being + V3` after prepositions.
- At least 20% active non-finite forms (`He hates interrupting people` vs `He hates being interrupted`).
- After like/hate/love/prefer both `to be told` and `being told` are correct; put both in `accepted_answers`.
- **Mistakes:** `to been told`, `being tell`, `without noticing` instead of `without being noticed`.
