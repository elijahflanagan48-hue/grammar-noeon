## D6. Two objects, prepositional and phrasal verbs (medium and advanced)

- **medium:** `I was given a key`, `We were told the news`, `She was offered a job`, `He was shown the room`; `The baby was looked after by…`, `The man was laughed at`, `The plan was talked about`. Verbs: give, tell, show, offer, send, ask, pay + look after, laugh at, talk about, wait for, pay for, look at, listen to.
- **advanced:** also `A key was given to me`, `be run over`, `be brought up`, `be put off`, `be taken care of`, `be dealt with`; the preposition stays after the verb.
- **Mistakes:** `was looked` (no after), `The bill was paid` (when the answer is `was paid for`), `It was told me`, `I was shown the room by`.
