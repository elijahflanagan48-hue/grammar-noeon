## D1. Tense of the passive (the core of the topic)

The learner knows the action is done *to* the subject and builds the right `be + V3`.

- **basic:** Present Simple, Past Simple, Future Simple (`will be`). One gap, one short sentence, ordinary things and people: `Our tickets (to send) yesterday.` → **were sent**. Mix the three tenses evenly. A time word is allowed only in 1 task of 3; elsewhere a second sentence or the situation shows the tense (a notice → Present Simple; a story → Past Simple; a plan → Future Simple).
- **medium:** add Present/Past Continuous and Present Perfect: `Please wait: the lift (to repair).` → **is being repaired**; `Have you (to tell) about the new rule?` → **been told**. Mix all six tenses.
- **advanced:** add Past Perfect and Future Perfect; two events in one sentence decide the tense.
- At least 30% of the tasks are about people (`I was told…`, `Have we been invited?`). At least 25% are negatives and questions.
- **Mistakes of Russian speakers (mc/fix):** `is build`, `was build` (no V3); `has been build`; `is repairing` instead of `is being repaired`; `will be finish`; `was/were` agreement with `news`, `police`, `people`.
