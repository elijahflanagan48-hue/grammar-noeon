## D11. Questions, negatives, word order

- **basic:** `Is the shop closed on Sundays?` `Was your phone found?` `When was the bridge opened?` `Who was this picture painted by?` `The tickets weren't sold.` Present and Past Simple, `will`.
- **medium:** Continuous and Perfect questions: `Has the room been cleaned yet?`, `Why was he not told?`, `Is the bus being repaired?`; short answers; place of `already / just / still / never`.
- **advanced:** `Who was it written by?` `What is it made from?`, `Haven't you been told?`, word order with adverbs (`was recently opened`, `has still not been found`).
- **Mistakes:** `Was opened the shop?`, `Did the bridge built?`, `Where the key was found?`, `Isn't been sent`.
