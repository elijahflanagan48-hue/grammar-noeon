## D7. get-passive and have something done (medium and advanced)

- **medium:** only the `get`-passive: `My phone got broken on the bus.` → **got broken**; `He got hurt at football.` → **got hurt**; `They get paid on Fridays.` → **get paid**. Never `get` with a long agent.
- **advanced:** `have/get + object + V3`: `We had the roof repaired.` / `She's going to get her hair cut.`; the choice between `I repaired it`, `I had it repaired`, `it was repaired`.
- Include `have` and `get` in `accepted_answers` when both are natural.
- **Mistakes:** `I cut my hair` (when a hairdresser did it), `have repaired the car`, `had repair the car`.
