## D10. by, with, and no agent

- **basic:** a choice between `by` (the doer: a person, a thing that acts) and `with` (a tool, a material): `The window was broken ___ a ball.` → **by**; `The soup was cooked ___ a big spoon`… prefer natural: `The letter was opened ___ a knife.` → **with**; `The room was filled ___ people.` → **with**. Also decide when an agent is not needed. Mostly `mc`.
- **medium and advanced:** also `made of / from / by`, `known for / as`, `covered with / in`, `filled with`, `interested in`, `surprised at / by`.
- **Mistakes:** `by a knife`, `made by wood`, `known with`, `covered by snow` (state).
