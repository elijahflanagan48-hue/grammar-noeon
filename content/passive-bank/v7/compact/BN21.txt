## D8. be allowed / asked / told / made to (medium) and make, see, hear (advanced)

- **medium:** `We were allowed to leave early.` `He was asked to wait.` `Children are not allowed to park here.` `I was told to call back.` Only `allowed, asked, told, made, invited, forced, taught`.
- **advanced:** `She was made to wait`, `He was seen to leave`, `They were heard shouting`; `let` has no passive (`was allowed to`).
- **Mistakes:** `were made wait` (no to), `was allowed leave`, `They were let to go`.
