## D1. Tense of the passive (the core of the topic)

The learner knows the action is done *to* the subject and builds the right `be + V3`.

- **basic:** Present Simple, Past Simple, Future Simple (`will be`). One gap, one short sentence, ordinary things and people: `Our tickets (to send) yesterday.` → **were sent**. Mix the three tenses evenly. A time word is allowed only in 1 task of 3; elsewhere a second sentence or the situation shows the tense (a notice → Present Simple; a story → Past Simple; a plan → Future Simple).
- **medium:** add Present/Past Continuous and Present Perfect: `Please wait: the lift (to repair).` → **is being repaired**; `Have you (to tell) about the new rule?` → **been told**. Mix all six tenses.
- **advanced:** add Past Perfect and Future Perfect; two events in one sentence decide the tense.
- At least 30% of the tasks are about people (`I was told…`, `Have we been invited?`). At least 25% are negatives and questions.
- **Mistakes of Russian speakers (mc/fix):** `is build`, `was build` (no V3); `has been build`; `is repairing` instead of `is being repaired`; `will be finish`; `was/were` agreement with `news`, `police`, `people`.

<!-- CELL D2 -->
## D2. Active or passive?

The bracket gives only the verb; the answer may be **active or passive**. **40–50% of all gaps in the batch are active.**

- **basic:** Present Simple and Past Simple only. Easy pairs: `The bus (to arrive) late, so we (to send) a message to the teacher.` → **arrived · sent**. Intransitive verbs (`arrive, happen, come, go, stay, start, end, fall, wait`) give active answers; a doer-less action gives passive: `My bag (to find) in the park.` → **was found**.
- **medium:** all tenses of basic and medium; one text may contain both voices of one verb (`The shop (to close) at six. Yesterday it (to close) early because of the snow.` → **closes · was closed**).
- **advanced:** include Perfect tenses and verbs where both voices are possible but only one fits the information order.
- The context must make the voice unambiguous. In `mini`, at least one gap is active and one is passive.
- **Mistakes (mc/fix):** `was happened`, `was arrived`, `was disappeared`, `is belonged to`, `The prices were risen`, `I was explained the rule`.

<!-- CELL D3 -->
## D3. Modal passive

- **basic:** only `can, must, should, will` + `be + V3`, present meaning. Rules and notices: `Phones (must / to switch off) in the exam.` → **must be switched off**; `Shoes (can / to leave) at the door.` → **can be left**. Include negatives (`can't be used`, `mustn't be opened`).
- **medium:** add `may, might, could, would, have to, had to, ought to`; present and future meaning. No perfect modals.
- **advanced:** perfect modals (`must/might/can't/should have been + V3`) and `will have to be`, `is going to have to be`; deduction and criticism about the past.
- The modal is in the bracket: `(must / to wear)`. At least 20% negatives.
- **Mistakes (mc/fix):** `must done`, `must to be done`, `should have be told`, `can't been seen`, `is going to repaired`.

<!-- CELL D4 -->
## D4. Infinitive and -ing forms (medium and advanced only)

- **medium:** `to be + V3` after want, need, hope, expect, would like, refuse; simple `being + V3` after like, hate, love, enjoy, without, before, after. The voice is decided by meaning: `Nobody likes (to tell) what to do.` → **to be told / being told**; `I want (to tell) the truth.` → **to tell** (active). Examples: `The children hope (to invite) to the party.` → **to be invited**.
- **advanced:** also `to have been + V3`, `having been + V3`, `being + V3` after prepositions.
- At least 20% active non-finite forms (`He hates interrupting people` vs `He hates being interrupted`).
- After like/hate/love/prefer both `to be told` and `being told` are correct; put both in `accepted_answers`.
- **Mistakes:** `to been told`, `being tell`, `without noticing` instead of `without being noticed`.

<!-- CELL D5 -->
## D5. Reporting passive (advanced only)

`It is said / believed / reported / thought / expected that…`; `He is said to be…`; `She is believed to have left…`; `The film is expected to open…`. News lines, notices, school and sport items. Simple vocabulary even though the construction is advanced.

- Mix `It is said that…` and `He is said to…` and the perfect infinitive (`to have won`).
- **Mistakes:** `It is said him to be`, `He is said that…`, `is believed leave`.

<!-- CELL D6 -->
## D6. Two objects, prepositional and phrasal verbs (medium and advanced)

- **medium:** `I was given a key`, `We were told the news`, `She was offered a job`, `He was shown the room`; `The baby was looked after by…`, `The man was laughed at`, `The plan was talked about`. Verbs: give, tell, show, offer, send, ask, pay + look after, laugh at, talk about, wait for, pay for, look at, listen to.
- **advanced:** also `A key was given to me`, `be run over`, `be brought up`, `be put off`, `be taken care of`, `be dealt with`; the preposition stays after the verb.
- **Mistakes:** `was looked` (no after), `The bill was paid` (when the answer is `was paid for`), `It was told me`, `I was shown the room by`.

<!-- CELL D7 -->
## D7. get-passive and have something done (medium and advanced)

- **medium:** only the `get`-passive: `My phone got broken on the bus.` → **got broken**; `He got hurt at football.` → **got hurt**; `They get paid on Fridays.` → **get paid**. Never `get` with a long agent.
- **advanced:** `have/get + object + V3`: `We had the roof repaired.` / `She's going to get her hair cut.`; the choice between `I repaired it`, `I had it repaired`, `it was repaired`.
- Include `have` and `get` in `accepted_answers` when both are natural.
- **Mistakes:** `I cut my hair` (when a hairdresser did it), `have repaired the car`, `had repair the car`.

<!-- CELL D8 -->
## D8. be allowed / asked / told / made to (medium) and make, see, hear (advanced)

- **medium:** `We were allowed to leave early.` `He was asked to wait.` `Children are not allowed to park here.` `I was told to call back.` Only `allowed, asked, told, made, invited, forced, taught`.
- **advanced:** `She was made to wait`, `He was seen to leave`, `They were heard shouting`; `let` has no passive (`was allowed to`).
- **Mistakes:** `were made wait` (no to), `was allowed leave`, `They were let to go`.

<!-- CELL D9 -->
## D9. State, action and the limits of the passive (medium and advanced)

- **medium:** `The shop is closed` (state) vs `is being closed` (action); `I was born in…` (never `I born`); `The car needs washing` / `needs to be washed`; verbs with no passive (`have, fit, suit, lack, resemble`).
- **advanced:** state vs action with `been`, `The door was locked when I got there`, `get/be + V3`, subtle choices.
- **Mistakes:** `I am born`, `The shirt is suiting you`, `The bag is belonged to me`, `needs wash`.

<!-- CELL D10 -->
## D10. by, with, and no agent

- **basic:** a choice between `by` (the doer: a person, a thing that acts) and `with` (a tool, a material): `The window was broken ___ a ball.` → **by**; `The soup was cooked ___ a big spoon`… prefer natural: `The letter was opened ___ a knife.` → **with**; `The room was filled ___ people.` → **with**. Also decide when an agent is not needed. Mostly `mc`.
- **medium and advanced:** also `made of / from / by`, `known for / as`, `covered with / in`, `filled with`, `interested in`, `surprised at / by`.
- **Mistakes:** `by a knife`, `made by wood`, `known with`, `covered by snow` (state).

<!-- CELL D11 -->
## D11. Questions, negatives, word order

- **basic:** `Is the shop closed on Sundays?` `Was your phone found?` `When was the bridge opened?` `Who was this picture painted by?` `The tickets weren't sold.` Present and Past Simple, `will`.
- **medium:** Continuous and Perfect questions: `Has the room been cleaned yet?`, `Why was he not told?`, `Is the bus being repaired?`; short answers; place of `already / just / still / never`.
- **advanced:** `Who was it written by?` `What is it made from?`, `Haven't you been told?`, word order with adverbs (`was recently opened`, `has still not been found`).
- **Mistakes:** `Was opened the shop?`, `Did the bridge built?`, `Where the key was found?`, `Isn't been sent`.
